Model card

What a model entry documents.

Every model entry answers the following. The name in code is the key used in the registry data and in the proposal form.

Urban question urban_question
The city problem the work addresses, and who is meant to use the result.
Intended task intended_task
The prediction, representation, or generation task, and the urban decision it is meant to inform.
Inputs & modalities inputs_modalities
Imagery, maps, mobility traces, text, tabular records, or sensor streams, with resolution and preprocessing.
Training data data_provenance
Sources, geography, time span, licence, sampling, and any data that was excluded and why.
Evaluation evaluation
Held-out tasks, metrics, baselines, and uncertainty estimates, with the evaluation code released.
Failure modes failure_modes
Observed errors, subgroup gaps, distribution shifts, and conditions under which outputs should not be used.
Geographic validity geographic_validity
Cities trained and tested on, and evidence for or against transfer to other places.
Usage constraints usage_constraints
Intended users, prohibited uses, and requirements for human review.
Artifacts & citation version license links citation
Code, configuration, weights or access route, version, licence, and citation.
