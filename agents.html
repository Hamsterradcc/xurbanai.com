What an agent entry documents.

Every agent entry answers the following. The name in code is the key used in the registry data and in the proposal form.

Urban question urban_question
The city problem the work addresses, and who is meant to use the result.
Task & workflow task_workflow
The research or planning task, the steps the agent performs, and where the workflow starts and stops.
Tools & data access data_provenance
Models, datasets, APIs, and documents the agent can call or read, with their licences.
Decision boundaries decision_boundaries
What the agent may decide alone, what requires a human, and how its outputs are reviewed.
Evaluation protocol evaluation
Success criteria, reliability across runs, comparison with human performance, and reproducibility.
Failure modes failure_modes
Observed errors, fabrication risks, bias in retrieved evidence, and misuse scenarios.
Traceability traceability
Logs, intermediate outputs, and citations the agent produces so that results can be audited.
Geographic validity geographic_validity
Cities tested, and whether claims are local or supported by cross-city evidence.
Usage constraints usage_constraints
Intended users, settings where the agent must not be used, and cost or rate considerations.
Artifacts & citation version license links citation
Code, prompts or policies, configuration, version, licence, and citation.
