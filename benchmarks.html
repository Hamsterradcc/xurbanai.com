What a benchmark entry documents.

Every benchmark entry answers the following. The name in code is the key used in the registry data and in the proposal form.

Research question urban_question
The urban capability the task measures, and why that capability matters for a decision.
Task definition task_definition
Inputs, expected outputs, and the exact instructions or prompts given to a system under test.
Data data_provenance
Source, geography, time span, licence, splits, and how test data is protected from leakage.
Metrics & criteria metrics
Scoring rules, what counts as success, and how uncertainty or disagreement is reported.
Baselines baselines
Reference systems, including simple and human baselines where they exist, with their scores.
Task validation evaluation
How the task itself was checked: annotation agreement, leakage tests, and whether scores track the capability it claims to measure.
Known limitations failure_modes
Ways the benchmark can be gamed or misread, and capabilities it does not measure.
Generalization protocol geographic_validity
How performance is tested across cities, periods, or populations beyond the development data.
Submission rules submission_rules
How results are submitted, what must be disclosed, and how evaluation code is run.
Versioning & errata versioning_errata
Version history, corrections to data or scoring, and how earlier results are handled.
Artifacts & citation version license links citation
Task files, evaluation code, version, licence, and citation.
