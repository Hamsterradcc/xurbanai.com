What a benchmark entry documents.

In addition to the six common fields, every benchmark entry answers the following.

Research question
The urban capability the task measures, and why that capability matters for a decision.
Task definition
Inputs, expected outputs, and the exact instructions or prompts given to a system under test.
Data
Source, geography, time span, licence, splits, and how test data is protected from leakage.
Metrics & criteria
Scoring rules, what counts as success, and how uncertainty or disagreement is reported.
Baselines
Reference systems, including simple and human baselines where they exist, with their scores.
Submission rules
How results are submitted, what must be disclosed, and how evaluation code is run.
Generalization protocol
How performance is tested across cities, periods, or populations beyond the development data.
Versioning & errata
Version history, corrections to data or scoring, and how earlier results are handled.
